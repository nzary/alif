## What it is

The Adoption Charter records an organization's decision to adopt ALiF. It is short: a dated commitment with named owners and defined parameters that can be cited in governance reports, compliance submissions to government and audits. The template is part of the [Institutional Adoption Guide]({{ '/companions/institutional-adoption-guide/' | relative_url }}).

The charter is produced at the end of the first phase of the [Implementation Pathway Model]({{ '/companions/implementation-pathway-model/' | relative_url }}) (readiness and commitment), approved by the senior leader responsible for strategic oversight, and reviewed every year by the governance body.

## What the charter records

| Field | What to enter |
|:---|:---|
| Organization | Name of the adopting organization |
| Date of adoption | The date the commitment takes effect |
| ALiF Standard version | The version adopted against |
| Adoption classification | Conforming or aligned |
| Conformance target date | If aligned, the date for full conformance, at most three years after adoption |
| Populations in scope | Stakeholder groups and approximate numbers |
| Strategic oversight | Name and title of the responsible senior leader |
| AI literacy coordinator | Name, title and share of time allocated |
| Main AI literacy program | The program the organization will mainly use |
| Signature | Signed and dated by the strategic oversight role |

## Additions in version 2.0

Version 2.0 of the Adoption Guide, planned with ALiF 2.0, will add two elements:

- **Learning Conditions Statement.** A clause committing the organization to publish and maintain a [Learning Conditions Statement]({{ '/standard/learning-conditions-statement/' | relative_url }}) for the people in scope: learning tasks affected by AI, practice and supervision, learning time, voice, and the commitments behind any decisions that use ALiF levels.
- **Decision rights on the use of results.** Named owners for how assessment results may and may not be used. Under the proposed ALiF 2.0 rule, self-assessment results, participation records beyond completion and AI-use data are not used in decisions about individuals such as hiring, progression, pay, discipline, privileges or placement.

## What the charter is not

- **Not a conformance declaration.** Conformance is claimed after the program has delivered; the charter records the commitment to deliver.
- **Not a policy.** The organization's AI literacy policy sets the obligations; the charter records the operating parameters.
- **Not a one-time document.** It is updated each year for changes in scope, coordinator, classification or target dates.
