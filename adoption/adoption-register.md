## What it is

The Adoption Register is where formal ALiF adoption events are logged. The core team maintains it, and it is cross-referenced in the [Architecture Document]({{ '/governance/architecture-document/' | relative_url }}). It is defined in the [Versioning and Review Policy]({{ '/governance/versioning-policy/' | relative_url }}) and the Architecture Document.

## What each entry records

- the adopting organization or jurisdiction;
- the date of formal adoption;
- the version of the ALiF Standard adopted against;
- the scope of adoption: which layers, components and stakeholder roles;
- any conditions or adaptations;
- the designated liaison for the organization, or the ALiF Hub that supports it;
- from ALiF 2.0, for organizational implementations, the date of the current [Learning Conditions Statement]({{ '/standard/learning-conditions-statement/' | relative_url }}).

## Why it matters

The register provides a way to check whether an organization's claim about ALiF has been recorded, and against which version. It also gives later adopters evidence of who has gone before them.

It has a governance role too. The number of jurisdictions in the register decides when governance of ALiF moves beyond the core team: when two or more jurisdictions have formally adopted the Standard, a [Governance Advisory Council]({{ '/adoption/governance-advisory-council/' | relative_url }}) must be set up. Requests from two or more adopting organizations or jurisdictions can also trigger a major review of the Standard.

## Current state

No conformance claim has been registered. Organizations that adopt ALiF record their commitment in an [Adoption Charter]({{ '/adoption/adoption-charter-template/' | relative_url }}); a conformance claim comes later, after the program has delivered and the evidence required by the [conformance requirements]({{ '/standard/conformance/' | relative_url }}) exists.

## Transition to ALiF 2.0

Entries record the Standard version they refer to, so claims made against version 1 remain readable after ALiF 2.0 is released. The proposed transition period and the timing of the new requirements are described on the [conformance]({{ '/standard/conformance/' | relative_url }}) and [ALiF 2.0]({{ '/standard/v2/' | relative_url }}) pages.
