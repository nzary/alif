<h2>The five components</h2><ul class="card-grid">
<li><a href="{{ '/standard/components/tec/' | relative_url }}">Technical Understanding (TEC)</a><span>Knowledge and skills required to understand AI systems and their capabilities.</span></li>
<li><a href="{{ '/standard/components/eva/' | relative_url }}">Critical Evaluation (EVA)</a><span>Systematic assessment of AI outputs, processes, and impacts.</span></li>
<li><a href="{{ '/standard/components/pra/' | relative_url }}">Practical Application (PRA)</a><span>Effective implementation of AI tools and processes in real-world contexts.</span></li>
<li><a href="{{ '/standard/components/eth/' | relative_url }}">Ethical Considerations (ETH)</a><span>Responsible and principled use of AI in professional contexts.</span></li>
<li><a href="{{ '/standard/components/dat/' | relative_url }}">Data Literacy (DAT)</a><span>Understanding data as the foundation for AI systems.</span></li>

<p>The components are interdependent. Data Literacy provides a foundation for the others, Technical Understanding and Critical Evaluation develop together, Ethical Considerations cuts across all components, and Practical Application draws on all of them at once.</p>
