<p>Technical Understanding is one of the five components of AI literacy in the ALiF Standard. Every conforming implementation addresses all five, with emphasis that varies by professional role.</p>
<h2>Key elements</h2><ul><li>AI principles and concepts</li><li>Tool operations and applications</li><li>Integration methods</li><li>Limitations and constraints</li></ul>
<h2>Example competencies</h2><ul><li>Explains AI concepts in context-appropriate terms</li><li>Identifies suitable AI tools for specific tasks</li><li>Integrates AI tools into professional workflows</li><li>Recognizes and articulates limitations of AI systems</li></ul>
<h2>How it relates to the other components</h2><p>Technical Understanding (TEC) and Critical Evaluation (EVA) as reciprocal. Understanding how AI tools work (TEC) informs the ability to evaluate their outputs (EVA), and the process of evaluating outputs deepens understanding of how tools function. These components develop most effectively when paired rather than sequenced.</p>
<h2>Sub-competencies (3)</h2>
<section class="subcomp" id="tec-1">
<h3>TEC.1 Conceptual Awareness</h3><p>The ability to understand and explain what AI systems are, how they function at a conceptual level, and where their capabilities and limitations lie. This sub-competency encompasses knowledge of different AI types and their applications, familiarity with core terminology, and the capacity to recognize which AI tools are appropriate for which tasks. It does not require understanding the mathematical or engineering foundations of AI systems.</p>

<section class="subcomp" id="tec-2">
<h3>TEC.2 Tool Proficiency</h3><p>The ability to use AI tools effectively to accomplish professional tasks. This sub-competency encompasses crafting effective prompts and inputs, navigating the features and settings of AI platforms, adapting outputs to meet specific needs, and troubleshooting common issues. It is assessed through demonstrated interaction with AI systems, not theoretical knowledge about them.</p>
<div class="table-wrap"><table><caption class="sr-only">Level descriptors for TEC.2</caption><thead><tr><th scope="col">Foundation (L1)</th><th scope="col">Intermediate (L2)</th><th scope="col">Advanced (L3)</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Writes basic prompts that produce relevant outputs. Combines multiple AI tools for complex tasks. Customizes tool settings to match specific needs. Develops reusable prompt templates.</td><td>Creates and validates systematic prompt methodologies. Develops integrated multi-tool workflows for organizational use. Designs training materials that enable others’ proficiency. Evaluates new tools against established effectiveness criteria.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
</section>
<section class="subcomp" id="tec-3">
<h3>TEC.3 Process Knowledge</h3><p>The ability to integrate AI tools into professional workflows and document that integration systematically. This sub-competency encompasses designing AI-enhanced processes, following established protocols for AI use, maintaining records of AI-assisted work, and understanding best practices for human-AI collaboration. It bridges individual tool use (TEC.2) and organizational implementation (PRA.1).</p>
