<tr><th scope="row">Foundation (L1)</th><td>Essential competencies and basic understanding</td><td><ul><li>Basic understanding of AI concepts and tools</li><li>Fundamental verification and evaluation skills</li><li>Essential practical applications within professional context</li><li>Core ethical awareness and compliance</li></ul></td><td>Follows established procedures. <p>Level transitions are meant to be qualitative shifts in what a person does, not the same activity done more intensively. Each sub-competency has its own descriptors at each level, shown on the component pages.</p>
<p>A person's profile across the sixteen sub-competencies is more informative than a single score. Uneven profiles are expected and show where development is needed.</p>
