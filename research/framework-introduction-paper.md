## What it is intended to be

The Framework Introduction Paper is planned as a peer-reviewed article that presents ALiF as a whole: its five components (TEC, EVA, PRA, ETH, DAT), sixteen sub-competencies, three progression levels and role-specific adaptation across learners, educators, researchers, clinicians and administrators. The target journal is Computers & Education.

## How it differs from other ALiF papers

- The [Concept Paper]({{ '/companions/concept-paper/' | relative_url }}) set out the original model, from which the Standard was consolidated.
- The [Protocol Paper]({{ '/research/alif-protocol-paper/' | relative_url }}) describes how the self-assessment questionnaire is being developed and validated.
- The [OLD conceptual article]({{ '/research/old-concept-paper/' | relative_url }}) covers the organizational conditions for AI literacy.

The introduction paper would be the reference article for the framework itself.

## Status

The paper is planned. No manuscript has been submitted, and no dates are set. Details will be published on this page when the paper is submitted.
