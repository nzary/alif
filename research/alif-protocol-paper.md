## What it is

The Protocol Paper is the public, citable version of the ALiF [Development Protocol]({{ '/companions/development-protocol/' | relative_url }}). It was published on Preprints.org in November 2024. It describes how the [ALiF self-assessment questionnaire]({{ '/instruments/alif-saq/' | relative_url }}) (ALiF-SAQ) was to be built and given its first validation, before full psychometric testing.

## What it sets out

- **Aim:** a self-assessment instrument for AI literacy across learners, educators, researchers, clinicians and administrative staff, based on the ALiF components and levels.
- **Design:** a pragmatic mixed-methods study in four phases over about three months: expert review (5 to 10 experts), cognitive testing (10 people, two per role), alpha testing (20 to 30), and launch inside a foundation course with opt-in research consent (target of 200 or more).
- **Dual profile:** the instrument reports a core competency profile and a role-specific profile.
- **Outcomes:** a working instrument, content and face validity, feasibility, internal consistency and user experience.
- **Deferred analyses:** confirmatory factor analysis and measurement invariance testing, which need larger samples, are left to later validation.
- **Ethics:** approval from an institutional review board before recruitment; taking the course never depends on research consent.

## Where it leads

The work the protocol describes continues as [Phase 1 of SAQ validation]({{ '/research/saq-validation/' | relative_url }}). The ALiF Standard cites the Development Protocol as the source of its development method. Under the Architecture Document, a preprint or peer-reviewed publication with a full psychometric report is part of the evidence needed for an instrument to reach the ALiF Core tier (see [endorsement tiers]({{ '/governance/endorsement-tiers/' | relative_url }})).
