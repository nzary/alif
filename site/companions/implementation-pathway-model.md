## What it is for

The Standard says what AI literacy is. The Implementation Pathway Model shows how an institution can build it: how to assess where people start, decide what to teach first, design pathways for different roles, and keep conformance up to date. It is informative guidance, not a set of requirements.

## Five phases of adoption

| Phase | Focus | Indicative duration | Output |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1. Readiness and commitment | Stakeholder alignment, governance, resources | 1 to 2 months | Adoption charter |
| 2. Baseline assessment | Self-assessment, context mapping, gap analysis | 1 to 3 months | Institutional profile |
| 3. Pathway design | Sequencing, program selection, resources, timeline | 1 to 2 months | Implementation plan |
| 4. Implementation | Delivery, ongoing assessment, adjustment | 12 to 24 months | Conformance evidence |
| 5. Sustained conformance | Reassessment, version migration, continuous development | Ongoing | Conformance renewal |

Durations depend on the size and infrastructure of the institution.

## Choosing what to teach first

The model offers three sequencing archetypes: **foundation first**, the recommended default; **risk-driven priority**, where high-stakes AI is already in use and ethics, evaluation and data literacy are delivered together; and **building from strength**, starting where a profile is already strong. It explains how to match assessment results to program entry points and how to move from aligned to conforming status within three years.

## Also covered

Role selection and role-specific descriptors; people who hold more than one role; the organizational conditions that affect whether individual learning takes hold, including organizational learning debt; periodic reassessment and version migration; worked scenarios for a large academic health system, a mid-size university and a government health authority; a pathway design worksheet and a conformance checklist.
