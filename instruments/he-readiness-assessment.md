## What it is

The HE Readiness Assessment is a self-report instrument for the higher education setting that appears in the ALiF measurement ecosystem at the **Referenced** tier. Like the [HP Readiness Survey]({{ '/instruments/hp-readiness-survey/' | relative_url }}) for health professionals, it is classified as **Complementary**: it addresses readiness to develop AI literacy rather than AI literacy itself, and it does not operationalize the ALiF components.

## How it relates to ALiF

Complementary instruments sit beside the [ALiF-SAQ]({{ '/instruments/alif-saq/' | relative_url }}), not in place of it. Readiness information can help coordinators interpret a competency profile and decide whether a learner needs support with motivation, confidence or organizational barriers before or alongside training.

## What Referenced status means

**ALiF Referenced** is the entry tier of the ALiF endorsement model. It rests on self-declared alignment with at least one ALiF component, recorded in a Quick Registry form that the core team checks for completeness only. It does not mean that ALiF has reviewed or validated the instrument. Moving to **ALiF Endorsed** requires expert content validity evidence, pilot data from at least 30 respondents and a full [registry entry]({{ '/governance/instrument-registry-template/' | relative_url }}).
